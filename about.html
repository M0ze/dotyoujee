01Why this practice exists

We turn compliance from an anxious audit obstacle into an operational moat.

As commerce moves to mobile money, API banking, cloud ERPs and digital citizen services, attackers have escalated against local targets. At the same time, NITA-U, Bank of Uganda, PDPO and UCC have introduced mandatory statutory frameworks.

Organisations end up stuck between opaque guidelines and technical tools that don't satisfy a local inspector. dotyoujee closes that gap. We speak the language of packet captures and policy schedules in the same conversation — and we leave behind evidence both audiences accept.

That means no borrowed playbooks from markets with different regulators, no hourly billing that punishes efficiency, and no junior team hidden behind a senior name.

03Professional rigour

Standards we work to, plainly stated.

Certification tracks and methodology alignment — not borrowed titles. Clients may request proof during scoping.

Offensive security

CEH and OSCP-aligned methodology for penetration testing, exploitation and privilege escalation. Certification work is ongoing and disclosed honestly.

ISMS & audit

ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Auditor alignment and CISA study. ITGC reviews, Annex A control mapping and evidence packs for NISF.

Local regulatory fluency

NISF 2026, DPPA 2019, BoU technology-risk guidelines and UCC directives. The NITA-U authorisation track is an active milestone.

Foundations

CompTIA Security+ foundation for security operations, risk management and incident handling.

Frameworks

NIST CSF 2.0 and CIS Benchmarks for prioritisation and hardening, mapped onto local statutory requirements.

Continuous learning

NITA-U circulars, UCC notices and BoU guidance are tracked as part of normal delivery — not read when an audit lands.
