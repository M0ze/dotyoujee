Our Founding Purpose

"We transform compliance from an anxious audit obstacle into an enduring operational moat."

As East Africa’s commerce moves decisively to mobile money, API banking, cloud ERPs, and digital citizen services, threat actors have escalated attacks against local targets. Meanwhile, Ugandan regulators — NITA-U, Bank of Uganda (BoU), PDPO, and UCC — have rightly introduced strict, mandatory statutory frameworks.

Organizations often find themselves trapped between opaque legal guidelines and technical security tools that don't satisfy local inspectors. dotyoujee solves this permanently. We speak the exact language of technical security operations and statutory regulatory compliance.
