## Local regulatory expertise

Our core differentiator is understanding **Uganda-specific** requirements alongside international standards:

- **NISF 2026** — National Information Security Framework for government and suppliers
- **Data Protection and Privacy Act (2019)** — PDPO registration, DPIAs, enforcement trends
- **Bank of Uganda** — Cyber and Technology Risk Management Guidelines
- **UCC** — Cybersecurity guidelines for telecom and communications sector
- **ISO/IEC 27001** — International ISMS benchmark we map to local frameworks
