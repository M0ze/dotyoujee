# BoU Cyber Risk Self-Assessment

A starter checklist for financial institutions mapping their controls to the Bank of Uganda (BoU) Cyber and Technology Risk Management Guidelines.

> **Disclaimer:** This is a self-assessment aid, not a substitute for a formal BoU compliance audit. Regulated institutions should engage qualified assessors for attestation.

---

## 1. Board & senior management oversight

- [ ] Board or equivalent receives regular cyber risk reports
- [ ] A senior executive is accountable for technology and cyber risk
- [ ] Cyber risk is integrated into enterprise risk management
- [ ] Budget is allocated for security tools, training, and assessments

## 2. Cyber risk governance

- [ ] Cyber risk policy approved at board/senior management level
- [ ] Roles and responsibilities for IT, security, and business units are defined
- [ ] Third-party technology risk is governed under a formal program
- [ ] Policy review cycle is at least annual

## 3. Identity & access management

- [ ] Multi-factor authentication for privileged and remote access
- [ ] Periodic access reviews for core banking and payment systems
- [ ] Segregation of duties for financial transaction processing
- [ ] Privileged account monitoring and session logging

## 4. Threat detection & response

- [ ] Security monitoring (SIEM or equivalent) for critical systems
- [ ] Documented incident response plan tested at least annually
- [ ] BoU and relevant authority notification procedures are defined
- [ ] Forensic capability exists (internal or retained third party)

## 5. Vulnerability & patch management

- [ ] Regular vulnerability scans of internet-facing and internal systems
- [ ] Penetration testing conducted at least annually
- [ ] Critical patches applied within defined SLAs
- [ ] Change management process covers security impact review

## 6. Data protection & resilience

- [ ] Customer PII encrypted at rest and in transit
- [ ] Backup and disaster recovery tested with documented RTO/RPO
- [ ] Business continuity plan covers cyber disruption scenarios
- [ ] DPPA compliance program aligned with BoU expectations

## 7. Third-party & outsourcing

- [ ] Due diligence performed on cloud and fintech partners
- [ ] Contracts include security, audit rights, and breach notification clauses
- [ ] Concentration risk from single technology vendors is assessed
- [ ] API and open banking integrations are security-tested

## 8. Awareness & culture

- [ ] Mandatory cyber training for all staff, including executives
- [ ] Simulated phishing exercises conducted periodically
- [ ] Developers receive secure coding guidance for in-house applications
