Scammer Payback Africa Initiative

In an era of rising digital fraud across the continent, dotyoujee is developing proactive methodologies to disrupt fraudulent ecosystems. We are not just reactive; we are architecting the future of regional cybersecurity intelligence.

Our Strategic Approach

The Scammer Payback Africa initiative focuses on high-impact threat modeling, forensic data analysis, and the creation of automated reporting pipelines. By mapping the infrastructure used by sophisticated scam operations, we aim to provide actionable intelligence to law enforcement and regulatory bodies, enabling them to neutralize threats at their core.

Why This Matters

Cyber fraud erodes trust in the digital economy. Our initiative bridges the gap between complex technical research and public sector enforcement, ensuring that local expertise is leveraged to protect SMEs, financial institutions, and vulnerable citizens from large-scale social engineering and technical scams.
