03 / THE PRACTICE

What I can honestly deliver today.

Small-scope IT work for Ugandan SMEs and NGOs. Fixed price, written scope, no mystery retainers.

IT baseline for SMEs

Asset inventory, account review, permission clean-up, backup and restore checks, and a written "here's what you actually have" report.

Email & DNS hardening

SPF, DKIM, DMARC, MFA rollout and mailbox rule audits — the unglamorous fixes that stop most real-world compromise.

Compliance starter pack

NISF and DPPA self-assessment checklists, data inventories and a prioritised list of gaps. Advisory, not certification.

Security awareness sessions

Practical, local-context training on phishing, mobile money fraud and safe handling of company data.

Hardened web delivery

Simple static sites with TLS, CSP and sensible headers. No 3D gimmicks sold as security.

Learning-in-public talks

Short sessions for teams and schools on how to learn IT and security with a real, honest curriculum.

Not sold here, yet

  • 24/7 SOC or managed detection — no such operation exists.
  • Accredited penetration testing or incident response retainer — in training, or delivered with a named partner and disclosed as such.
  • Certifications I have not earned. When they exist, they get a verifiable ID and 500 XP.

04 / THE RULES

Why the points mean something.

The scoreboard is generated from committed files, not typed into a page. That's the whole trick.

No receipt, no XP

A lab, CTF, writeup or certification post must carry a working evidence URL. Otherwise it logs as zero, publicly.

Modules pay once

Each curriculum module pays out to the earliest post that claims it. You cannot farm the same lesson twice.

Commits are counted

The build script queries the GitHub API for real commits on the configured learning repos, capped at 40 XP per week.

Ranks aren't credentials

Recruit → Architect are game levels. They never appear as job titles or proof of competence.

Gaps stay visible

Streaks show breaks honestly. An empty dashboard says nothing has been proven yet, which is the truth.

You can audit it

Everything comes from journal/*.md, data/tracks.json and scripts/build.py in this repo.
