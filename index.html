About NexUpTech

NexUpTech is a self-taught IT and cybersecurity consultancy based in Kampala, Uganda. We combine hands-on technical skills with deep understanding of local regulations — NISF, UCC, BoU, and the Data Protection and Privacy Act — to help organizations stay audit-ready without enterprise-level budgets.

Our team pursues industry credentials including CEH, CompTIA Security+, CISA, and ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Auditor training. We are working toward NITA-U authorization to bid on government projects — a key milestone for serving public-sector clients.

Our positioning: Uganda's local compliance and security expert. We align local regulatory requirements with international best practices so you can compete confidently in the digital economy.
