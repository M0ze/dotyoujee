Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our services and processes

What is the National Information Security Framework (NISF) 2026?

The NISF 2026 is Uganda’s mandatory cybersecurity framework for government ministries, departments, and agencies (MDAs). It adopts a risk‑based approach with 14 control families covering governance, risk management, asset protection, incident response, and more. While legally binding for MDAs, many private sector vendors (especially those supplying government) are required to demonstrate compliance to win contracts.

How long does a typical VAPT engagement take?

Timeline depends on scope. A basic external network scan and web application test for a medium‑sized business usually takes 5‑10 business days. Larger environments with internal testing, wireless assessment, or social engineering may extend to 2‑3 weeks. We provide a detailed schedule during scoping.

Do you provide remediation support after delivering a report?

Yes. Our reports include prioritized remediation steps, and we offer optional remediation‑support packages where our engineers work with your IT team to implement fixes, validate patches, and conduct retesting to ensure vulnerabilities are closed.

Are your consultants certified?

Our team holds certifications including Offensive Security Certified Professional (OSCP), Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH), ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Implementer, and Certified Information Privacy Professional/Africa (CIPP/A). We maintain continuous professional development to stay current with evolving threats and regulations.
