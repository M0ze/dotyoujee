//The fine print, plainly

How buying actually works.

No hourly surprises, no auto-renewing mystery retainers, no "we'll get back to you" limbo.

Payment

50% at Statement of Work signing, 50% on acceptance. Bank transfer or Mobile Money merchant payment (MTN MoMo, Airtel Money). Retainers are billed monthly and cancel with 30 days' notice.

Guarantee

Every assessment tier includes a 30-day free retest of remediated findings. If a finding doesn't close, we retest at no extra cost and tell you exactly why.

Boundaries

Testing happens only under signed written authorisation. We do not scan targets we are not authorised for, and active incidents move to the DFIR hotline rather than the standard queue.

Scope changes

If you add assets mid-engagement, we stop, re-scope in writing and agree any price change before continuing. No silent overages.

What you own

Reports, evidence packs, scripts written for your environment and remediation roadmaps are yours. We don't hold your evidence hostage.

Data handling

Briefs and findings are handled under our DPPA-aligned privacy notice. Encrypted disclosure is available on request for sensitive material.
