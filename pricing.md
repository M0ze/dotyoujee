# dotyoujee Pricing

Transparent, productized pricing aligned with the Ugandan market. All final quotes are issued in Ugandan Shillings (UGX); USD figures on the website are indicative at UGX 3,700/$.

The live, detailed package ladder is at [`pricing.html`](pricing.html).

## Service tiers

| Tier | Name | Reference price (UGX) | Primary deliverables |
|------|------|----------------------|----------------------|
| 0 | Recon Call | Free | 20–30 min scoping call, free checklists, honest recommendation on fit |
| 1 | Essential Scan | 1,000,000 (one-off) | External port/TLS/header scan, CVE cross-reference, DPPA quick-check, PDF + JSON, 1-hour debrief |
| 2 | Guard Retainer | From 1,800,000 / month | Monthly attack-surface scanning, prioritised remediation queue, M365/Workspace baseline review, quarterly phishing sim, monthly posture report |
| 3 | Assurance Audit | 3,500,000 – 5,000,000 | ITGC audit (ISO 27001 aligned), web + network VAPT with PoCs, NISF 2026 gap analysis, DPIA, PDPO filing support, 30-day retest |
| 4 | Compliance Programme | 6,000,000 – 9,000,000 | Tier 3 plus BoU/UCC/NITA-U mapping, cloud posture review, social engineering, IR plan + tabletop, policy suite, advisory vCISO |
| 5 | Enterprise Domain | Custom (from 12,000,000) | Red team, 24/7 co-managed detection & response, embedded vCISO, DFIR retainer with SLA, regional threat intel, board reporting |

Each tier adds a capability; nothing is locked behind a worse tier than the one that needs it.

## What's included in every engagement

- Written report with executive summary
- Prioritised remediation recommendations
- Direct WhatsApp access to the responsible specialist
- Scope and price fixed in writing before invoicing

Assessments (Tiers 1 and 3–5) include a 30-day free retest of remediated findings.

---

## Payment terms

- **One-off engagements:** 50% at Statement of Work signing, 50% on acceptance
- **Retainers:** billed monthly, cancel with 30 days' notice
- **Enterprise:** milestone-based invoicing per statement of work
- Payment via MTN MoMo, Airtel Money merchant or bank transfer (details on quote)
- Third-party registrar, cloud or auditor fees are excluded and disclosed before purchase
