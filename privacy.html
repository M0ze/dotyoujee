Privacy

dotyoujee is a Ugandan consultancy. We handle any personal data you share with care.

What we collect

When you contact us through the website form or WhatsApp, we collect the information you provide — name, email, message, and services of interest — so we can respond to your enquiry.

How we use it

We use your data only to reply to your enquiry, deliver the service you requested, and (where you opt in) to send occasional newsletter updates.

Where your data lives

Email and WhatsApp messages are stored with our email/chat providers, which may be hosted outside Uganda. If you are concerned about where your data is processed, please contact us before submitting.

Your rights

Under Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act (DPPA), you may ask us to correct, restrict, or delete your personal data. Contact us at the details below.
