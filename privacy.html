Legal

Privacy, plainly.

What this site stores

Almost nothing. Progress data comes from public Markdown commits in this repository. There are no analytics, no tracking cookies, no third-party fonts and no advertising pixels.

When you book

The briefing form keeps your details in your browser until you choose to send them by WhatsApp or email. We do not silently store them on a server. If you send them, we use them only to prepare the scope and quote you asked for.

Third-party links

Evidence links in the journal point to places like GitHub, TryHackMe or certification bodies. Those sites have their own privacy terms.

Your choices

Ask for a copy or deletion of anything you sent us, and we will help. Contact details are on the booking page.
