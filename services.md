## Core compliance packs

### NISF 2026 compliance audit
**Target:** Government ministries, departments, agencies (MDAs), and their technology vendors.

**Deliverables:**
- Gap analysis against the National Information Security Framework (NISF) 2026
- Risk-ranked findings report with remediation roadmap
- Executive summary for leadership and audit committees
- Optional follow-up validation session

**Ideal for:** Organizations responding to mandatory NISF adoption across Uganda's public sector.

---

### Data protection compliance
**Target:** Any organization handling personal data — SMEs, NGOs, fintech, e-commerce, healthcare.

**Deliverables:**
- Assessment against the Data Protection and Privacy Act (2019)
- PDPO (Personal Data Protection Office) registration guidance
- Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) templates and review
- Privacy policy and consent mechanism recommendations

**Ideal for:** Companies preparing for enforcement actions or customer trust audits.
