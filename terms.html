Legal

Terms, without theatre.

Written scope first

Every engagement starts with a short written scope: what is in, what is out, the timeline and the fixed UGX price. Work begins after you accept it.

Authorisation is not optional

Technical testing only happens against systems you own or have written permission to test. No anonymous third-party scans, ever.

Payment

Small engagements: 50% on signing the scope, 50% on delivery and acceptance. Mobile Money or bank transfer. If the scope changes, we stop and re-scope in writing before any extra charge.

What you own

Reports, evidence packs and roadmaps written for you are yours on final payment. I keep ownership of generic scripts and the learning material published on this site.

Honest limits

No security review can promise zero future compromise. No claim on this site is a certification unless it carries a verifiable ID. Ugandan law governs these terms.
