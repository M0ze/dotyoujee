Commercial & Legal Terms

Terms of Service & Rules of Engagement

Governing all advisory engagements, penetration testing scopes, and automated security scans performed by dotyoujee in Uganda.

1. Engagement Scope & Legal Authorization

dotyoujee provides offensive and defensive cybersecurity advisory services. Technical testing, vulnerability scanning, and penetration testing are executed only upon explicit written authorization from the legal owner of the targeted systems. Unauthorized testing of third-party systems is unlawful under the Computer Misuse Act (Uganda) and will be refused immediately.

2. Rules of Engagement (RoE) & Safe Harbor

Prior to any offensive testing (VAPT), a mutual Rules of Engagement (RoE) document must be executed defining testing windows, IP whitelists, emergency contacts, out-of-scope production databases, and communication protocols. Testing is non-destructive by design; denial-of-service (DDoS) testing is strictly excluded unless explicitly commissioned under isolated sandbox conditions.

3. Fixed-Price Commercial Terms

Prices published on this site in Ugandan Shillings (UGX) reflect scoped packages (Starter, Professional, Enterprise). Custom engagements require a signed Statement of Work (SoW). Standard commercial terms require 50% advance deposit upon signing, and 50% upon delivery and presentation of the final audit report. All work includes a complimentary 30-day verification retest.

4. Intellectual Property & Deliverables

Upon settlement of invoices, the client receives exclusive, irrevocable rights to all customized deliverable reports, remediation roadmaps, and evidence files. dotyoujee retains ownership of its proprietary scripts, testing harnesses, and anonymized threat intelligence heuristics.

5. Limitation of Liability

While our assessments follow leading industry standards (ISO 27001, PTES, OWASP), no technical evaluation can guarantee 100% immunity from future zero-day exploits or determined state-sponsored adversaries. To the maximum extent permitted under Ugandan law, dotyoujee’s aggregate liability arising out of any engagement shall not exceed the total fees received for the specific service rendered.

6. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

These terms and all service agreements are governed by the laws of the Republic of Uganda. Any dispute shall be resolved through good-faith mediation in Kampala before recourse to the High Court of Uganda (Commercial Division).
